# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Critique the science, not the scientist.

## What this project is

HydroTuring exists to find out whether models conserve what physics says they
must. That means most results here are negative results about somebody's
work, sometimes work they are proud of. Two consequences.

**If you contribute a probe**, you are writing a test that will fail other
people's models. Write it so the failure is informative and reproducible, not
so it is embarrassing. Report the residual, not a verdict on the authors.

**If your model fails**, that is data, not an accusation. `FAIL` with reason
`INCOMPLETE` is the current state of nearly every published rainfall-runoff
model and says nothing about its predictive skill. A benchmark where nobody
is willing to submit a failing model is a benchmark that has stopped working.

Disagreement about tolerances, denominators and what counts as a violation is
expected and welcome. That is the scientific content of this project, and it
belongs in the open, in issues and pull requests.

## Unacceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about a person or their
institution. Publishing others' private information. Sustained disruption.
Presenting another person's probe or model as your own work.

Using a benchmark result to attack an individual rather than to characterise
a model. "This architecture cannot close the water balance" is a finding.
"These authors do not understand hydrology" is not, and will be moderated.

## Scope

Applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and when representing
the project at conferences and workshops.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer at chrimerss@gmail.com. Reports are handled
confidentially. If a report concerns the maintainer, contact any member of
the scientific steering committee listed in GOVERNANCE.md.
